How to play

Read this once before you begin

1️⃣

Nothing here is graded

There is no time limit and no penalty for a wrong answer. If you get something wrong, the activity will explain why — that explanation is the actual lesson.

2️⃣

Click anything you like

You cannot break these activities by clicking. Try an answer, read the explanation, then restart and try again — your score is kept so you can see yourself improve.

3️⃣

Talk before you click

If you are in a group, say your answer out loud and explain why before anyone clicks. Explaining the reason is what makes the idea stick.

Suggested order

If this is your first time, play them in the order shown: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5. Each one uses the idea from the one before it, so the maze teaches you how to think in steps before Find the Bug asks you to find a step that is wrong.
